# IMAGINE INDIA @ 2047 — Content & Creative Plan

**A campaign microsite by Emaginations**
*Independence Day interactive experience — quiz → personalised superpower → build your India → shareable card*

---

## Brand World

**Tone:** warm, playful, a little poetic — never corporate, never preachy. Speaks to Gen Z/young India like a clever friend, not a government pamphlet.

**Palette:** cream/off-white background, Emaginations blue, saffron, green (used sparingly), one warm accent (marigold or coral) for highlights.

**Visual language:** soft flat 2D illustration, oversized rounded typography, generous whitespace, small floating doodles (stars, sparks, leaves) drifting in the background, subtle parallax on scroll/hover.

**Recurring character: THE BULB** — a small glowing bulb-creature (Emaginations' mascot) that appears in a corner of every screen, reacting to what's happening: it flickers awake on landing, tilts its head during the quiz, lights up fully at the result reveal, and multiplies into tiny sparks during the Build India screen.

## Screen 2 — The Quiz

**Visual concept:**
One question per screen, presented as a large illustrated card with a short prompt at top and five answer tiles below, each with a small icon and a short evocative phrase (not a single word — these are meant to be savored, not skimmed). As the visitor picks an answer, that tile glows and gently expands before transitioning to the next question. A thin progress line at the top fills as they go (1 of 5 → 5 of 5) — not a percentage, just a visual sense of how far there is to go. A sixth screen appears only for the small number of visitors whose top score is still tied after question five — it should look and feel distinctly different from the five questions before it: only two tiles instead of five, a returning illustration of the same midnight flag-raising scene from Q1 (now slightly changed — the flag is caught halfway up, frozen), and a slower, weightier transition into it. This is the one moment in the whole quiz that should feel like a real decision rather than a preference.

Each question is grounded in a specific image or moment from India's independence — not abstract hypotheticals — but none of the options name the trait they map to. The visitor should feel like they're answering something true about a memory or a feeling, not picking a personality label. All three questions carry the full set of five options, one per archetype, so scoring stays clean and every archetype gets equal footing on every question.

**Q1**

> ### It's midnight, August 14th, 1947. You're standing in the crowd as the flag is about to rise for the first time in a free country. What can you not look away from?

- 🛠️ **The hands that stitched the last flag together, just in time** *(Builder)*
- 💡 **The strange quiet right before something no one's ever seen begins** *(Imaginer)*
- 🤝 **Strangers holding onto each other — some crying, some cheering, all at once** *(Connector)*
- 🔍 **The road leading out of the city, and where it might go now** *(Explorer)*
- 🌱 **The faces at the very back, still straining to get close enough to see** *(Changemaker)*

**Q2**

> ### A letter arrives from the year 2047, addressed to India in 1947 — but somehow, only you can read it. Which line makes you stop?

- 🛠️ **"Everything they only dreamed of, someone eventually built."** *(Builder)*
- 💡 **"The strangest ideas turned out to be the true ones."** *(Imaginer)*
- 🤝 **"No one changed the country alone — not once."** *(Connector)*
- 🔍 **"There was always somewhere further to go."** *(Explorer)*
- 🌱 **"Nobody was left standing outside, waiting, anymore."** *(Changemaker)*

**Q3**

> ### Your grandmother hands you a small, unlabeled tin box — something she carried with her through Partition. What do you hope is inside?

- 🛠️ **Tools, worn smooth from years of use** *(Builder)*
- 💡 **A blank notebook, page one, never written on** *(Imaginer)*
- 🤝 **A photograph of a table full of people she loved** *(Connector)*
- 🔍 **A map, with the places she never reached marked in pencil** *(Explorer)*
- 🌱 **A list of names — people she promised she'd never forget** *(Changemaker)*

**Q4**

> ### You're in a village square in 1947, packed shoulder to shoulder around a single radio, as Nehru's voice announces that India has "awoken to life and freedom." What do you find yourself doing, without quite realizing it?

- 🛠️ **Already thinking of the first thing you'd fix, tomorrow morning** *(Builder)*
- 💡 **Trying to picture a country that only exists in sound, so far** *(Imaginer)*
- 🤝 **Reaching for the hand of whoever's standing next to you** *(Connector)*
- 🔍 **Wondering how far this voice is traveling right now — how many villages, how many miles** *(Explorer)*
- 🌱 **Looking around to see who isn't there to hear it** *(Changemaker)*

**Q5**

> ### You're one of thousands walking beside Gandhi on the salt march to Dandi — day sixteen, feet aching, still 24 days from the sea. What keeps you moving?

- 🛠️ **Knowing exactly what you'll do the moment you reach the water** *(Builder)*
- 💡 **Still not quite believing an idea this simple could shake an empire** *(Imaginer)*
- 🤝 **The person beside you, who hasn't stopped talking since dawn** *(Connector)*
- 🔍 **Not knowing what's around the next bend — and needing to find out** *(Explorer)*
- 🌱 **Thinking of everyone back home who couldn't make this walk themselves** *(Changemaker)*

**Scoring note (content logic, not code):** each answer across all five questions maps 1:1 to one of the five archetypes. Each selection adds 1 point to that archetype's running tally. The archetype with the highest score becomes the visitor's Yuva Shakti result.

---

### How Scoring Works

Every question offers exactly one option per archetype. Each selection adds **1 point** to that archetype's running tally. After all five questions:

- Each archetype's score ranges from **0 to 5**.
- The archetype with the **highest score** becomes the visitor's Yuva Shakti result.
- Five questions (rather than three) is the important change here — it makes a clean 5-way tie almost impossible, and shrinks how often even a 2-way tie happens.

**When a tie still happens**: give the visitor one deliberate, forced choice between only the two archetypes they're torn between. It returns to the exact scene from Question 1 and asks them to decide, not just watch.

> ### The clock reaches midnight. The flag is halfway up. This time, it's your call to make.
>
>
> Same night. Same crowd. Same minute before midnight, August 14th, 1947 — except this time, someone turns to you and asks what happens next. There's no time left to weigh it.
>
>
> **What do you do?**

Only the two lines belonging to the visitor's tied archetypes are shown. Whichever line the visitor chooses wins the tie outright.

## Screen 4 — Build Your India @ 2047

**Visual concept:**
An empty, softly illustrated landscape — hills, a horizon line, open sky — sits at the top of the screen, waiting to be filled. Below it, 8 tiles (icon + one-line label). As the visitor taps each one (up to 3), an illustrated element animates into the landscape above in real time, so the "world" visibly grows with every choice. The Bulb sits at the edge, sprinkling a few sparks onto each newly added element.

**Header copy:**

> ## Now it's your turn to build.
>
>
> **Pick 3 things you want to see flourish in India by 2047.**

**The 8 tiles:**

Icon | Label | Landscape element it adds
---|---|---
🌱 | A Greener India | Trees and hills fill in across the horizon
💡 | An Innovative India | Small futuristic structures rise up
🎨 | A Creative India | Murals and art installations appear
📚 | A Knowledge-Rich India | A glowing library / open book motif appears
🤝 | A Connected India | Tiny figures appear, linked hand to hand
🚀 | An Explorative India | A rocket lifts off in the distance
❤️ | An Inclusive India | Warm light spreads evenly across the whole scene
🏙️ | A Future-Ready India | Clean geometric skyline silhouettes appear

**On selecting the 3rd tile, landscape complete:**

> **You just imagined a little piece of India 2047.**
>
>
> *Somewhere, a version of this is already being built.*
>
>
> [ See my card → ]

## Screen 5 — Your Shareable Card & Link

**Visual concept:**
Two things get generated together: a vertical card (1080×1920, for saving/posting to stories) and a **personal shareable link** — something like `imagineindia2047.in/i/imaginer` — unique to the visitor's archetype. When that link is pasted into WhatsApp, Instagram, or X, the link preview itself shows the visitor's personalized archetype imagery and CTA copy, so the invitation to "find yours" travels with the link before anyone even clicks it. Each of the 5 archetypes has its own preview image, its own accent color, and its own version of the CTA line below — so a Builder's link looks and reads differently from an Imaginer's.
